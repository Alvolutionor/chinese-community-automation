# WeChat: public accounts, Channels, and groups

## Discover

Keep these surfaces separate. Accessible public-account articles and public
Channels discussions can supply public evidence. An article URL does not guarantee
that comments are visible, searchable, or open to replies. Channels video content
needs actual accessible context, not an inferred title summary.

For closed groups, use user-supplied excerpts or a narrowly scoped, already
authorized content source. Ask which group and what content is in scope if needed.
Do not enumerate unrelated chat history, obtain invitations, join groups, or send
member requests. Group conversation text must not enter external search queries.
Do not promise web-wide search of WeChat group conversations or Channels comments.

## Draft

Answer the actual public comment or scoped group request. Match the existing
conversation and identify the user's material connection when recommending their
product. Do not collect participant contacts, add strangers, mass-message members,
or offer a "reply keyword to get resources" funnel unasked.

## Platform-specific checks

Read current public-platform rules, Channels rules, or group-admin rules for the
chosen surface. Group membership does not authorize marketing, forwarding, or
private solicitation. Public-account publishing capabilities do not imply a
general comment-posting API. If AI or commercial labels apply, report the observed
requirements rather than assuming identical rules across all WeChat surfaces.
