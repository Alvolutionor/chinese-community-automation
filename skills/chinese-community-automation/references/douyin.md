# Douyin

## Discover

Search videos matching the actual problem and inspect specific comment requests.
Verify relevant spoken/video content through an accessible transcript, subtitles,
description, or user-supplied evidence. A creator's demonstration and a viewer's
purchase request are different signals. Record the video and exact comment locator.

## Draft

Use a short, concrete answer to the viewer's question. Do not make unsupported
claims about the demonstrated product or pretend to have watched inaccessible
content. Disclose affiliation when an allowed recommendation is made. Do not use
repeated comments or contact-detail workarounds to create a sales funnel.

## Platform-specific checks

Inspect current community and surface-specific rules. Merchant, creator-partner,
paid-ad, and ordinary-comment rules have different scope. Confirm permitted product
mentions, transaction paths, and AI labels before treating a commercial draft as
ready for review. Unknown rules mean research or help-only drafting without links.
