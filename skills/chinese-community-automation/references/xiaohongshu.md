# Xiaohongshu / XHS / rednote

## Discover

Search concrete use cases and constraints, then inspect both the note and the
specific help requests in its comments. A note teaching a workflow is not itself a
buyer request; a commenter asking how to fix a particular problem may be one.
Record the note link and a visible comment locator. Keep `xsec_token` or other
parameters needed to open a supplied link; never invent tokens or strip them
blindly. Do not expose authentication cookies or session credentials.

Native-search query shapes: "任务 + 求推荐", "任务 + 怎么解决", "现有工具 + 替代",
"场景 + 预算/设备/导出限制". A `site:xiaohongshu.com` search can identify possible
notes, but snippets do not establish access to comments or freshness.

## Draft

For a comment, answer one specific point in one short paragraph, expanding only
when needed. Use natural Chinese matching the discussion, without forcing slang,
emoji, or stock promotional hooks. Mention a relevant limitation when recommending
an affiliated product. Do not pretend to be a satisfied ordinary customer.

If an original note is requested, structure it around a real problem, a useful
method or evidenced demonstration, and limitations. A cover brief or picture list
is a plan, not proof that screenshots or images have been created.

## Platform-specific checks

The official community convention asks for authentic sharing, transparent
commercial participation, and no fabricated personas or word-of-mouth. It advises
disclosing AI assistance and discourages non-official off-platform transaction
diversion. Check the applicable current rules and any professional-account or
partnership requirements for this specific activity.

Keep commercial affiliation visible in the text. Report applicable AI-label
guidance separately from that disclosure. Do not invent a universal penalty or a
mandatory label for every comment from a recommendation in the convention.
Do not add disguised contact details, homepage/DM bait, or an external purchase
path as a workaround. Merchant, paid-ad, and creator-partnership rules have their
own scope and must not be generalized to every organic reply.
