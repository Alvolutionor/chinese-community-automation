# Douban

## Discover

Use relevant groups and specific discussions, not unrelated book or movie reviews.
Read group descriptions, pinned rules, membership conditions, the original request,
and existing replies. Group-specific constraints can be stricter than the site-wide
rules. Do not join a group or inspect private member content as part of discovery.

## Draft

Use plain, restrained language and answer the person's stated practical need.
Do not insert a shopping pitch into unrelated personal discussion. When allowed
and relevant, disclose your real connection to a recommendation. Otherwise offer
help without promotion or exclude the thread.

## Platform-specific checks

The official guidelines address off-topic group content, spam, marketing diversion,
manufactured engagement, and machine-generated bulk activity. A group allowing
buying/selling has not necessarily allowed external sellers or promotional replies.
Inspect the exact local rules before drafting an affiliated recommendation.
