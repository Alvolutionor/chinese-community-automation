# SSPAI / Shaoshupai

## Discover

Look for workflow obstacles and product questions in relevant article discussions.
Search tasks, platforms, and constraints, not just the product's category. Read
the article's relevant method and existing comments so the reply can contribute
something specific rather than restate the article.

## Draft

Explain a useful workflow improvement or deciding tradeoff in restrained Chinese.
Recommendations need real supporting facts and transparent affiliations.
For an original article explicitly requested by the user, consult the author
manual and current review rules. Show actual methodology and evidence; do not
invent a personal productivity experiment or claim editorial acceptance.

## Platform-specific checks

The review rules describe conditions for promotional works, including appropriate
interest-related submission classification and sponsorship disclosure where
applicable. These are article-review rules, not blanket permission for promotional
comments. The style guide distinguishes requirements from recommendations. Remove
unnecessary tracking parameters without damaging resource-identifying URLs.
