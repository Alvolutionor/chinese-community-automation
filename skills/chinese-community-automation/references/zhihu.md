# Zhihu

## Discover

Search questions phrased around a decision or obstacle, including "有什么推荐",
"如何解决", "有什么替代", and relevant constraints. Inspect the question details,
dates, existing answers, and the specific comment being considered. Distinguish an
old question with recent unresolved comments from one that already has an adequate
answer. A high follower count does not establish present demand.

## Draft

An answer should state the useful conclusion, explain the deciding criteria, and
provide evidence or a concrete method. A comment should address its immediate
parent rather than become an unrelated mini-article. Name factual alternatives
when they matter and acknowledge the affiliated product's limitations. Separate
documented behavior from personal experience and avoid a fabricated expert persona.

## Platform-specific checks

Inspect current community management rules and any account-specific restrictions.
The official creator manual describes handling spam and malicious behavior; the
institutional-account rules address induced engagement and malicious private
marketing. Apply institutional rules only to institutional accounts. Product,
paid-content, and commercial tools do not establish permission to promote in an
arbitrary answer or comment. Unknown rules mean help-only drafting without links.
