# Bilibili

## Discover

Search relevant tutorials, reviews, articles, and dynamic posts, then inspect
specific requests in the comments. Verify enough video content through accessible
subtitles, description, or a transcript to understand the request. Record the
video/post URL and a visible comment locator; the title alone is insufficient.
Creators discussing a tool are not automatically asking for an alternative.

## Draft

Reply to the comment's actual question and, where relevant, the demonstrated step
or timestamp. Keep ordinary comments concise; expand a technical explanation when
the question requires it. Disclose affiliation without imitating a fan testimonial.
Do not promote through unrelated danmaku, repeated comments, or competitor attacks.

## Platform-specific checks

The official convention emphasizes real information and respectful discussion.
Inspect current comment rules and commercial-content requirements. A commercial
video partnership is different from an organic reply under someone else's video.
Do not apply the legacy `bbq.bilibili.com` product rules to the main Bilibili site.
If a video or post is requested, check current source and AI-label requirements
through the actual creator interface or official notices.
