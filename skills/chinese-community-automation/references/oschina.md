# OSCHINA

## Discover

Look at relevant open-source projects, technical Q&A, articles, and discussions.
Search a real implementation problem, missing capability, or alternative request.
Distinguish news about a project's release from a user asking for help. Verify the
project license, maintenance status, and capabilities when those facts matter.

## Draft

Offer a technical explanation, integration check, or appropriately evidenced
alternative. State maintainership or commercial involvement honestly. Do not call
a closed-source or merely free product open source. Avoid a reply that substitutes
marketing language for an answer to the technical question.

## Platform-specific checks

The official rules address improper marketing, misleading promotion, private-message
marketing, coordinated question/answer campaigns, and manufactured engagement.
Read the relevant section for the exact surface; an open-source identity does not
exempt a product from marketing rules. Where promotion is unsupported, provide
help-only content or research findings.
