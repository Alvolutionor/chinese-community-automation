# Weibo

## Discover

Search actual recommendation and problem-solving requests. Read the original post,
timestamp, conversation, and requester, rather than using an unrelated trending
topic for exposure. A repost may be commentary instead of the original request.
Record which author's statement establishes the need.

## Draft

Address one concrete point with a short reply. Do not attach unrelated trending
hashtags, tag uninvolved accounts, impersonate a customer, or speculate about a
competitor's quality. Any affiliated recommendation needs direct disclosure and
permission on that surface; otherwise keep it help-only.

## Platform-specific checks

The official convention addresses unauthorized abnormal-frequency automation,
data collection, spam, repeated comments, misleading engagement, diversion, and
unrelated trending-topic use. Ordinary reading is different from bulk collection.
Use permitted public search or an authorized interface, with no scraping around
blocks. Do not assume a link is permitted because the interface accepts its text.
