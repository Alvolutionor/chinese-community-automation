# LINUX DO: research and rule review

## Supported scope

Find accessible, genuine needs in relevant topics and summarize product fit for
the user. Read the opening post, replies, category, tags, dates, and current rules.
Respect invitation, trust-level, and category access boundaries. Do not obtain
invitations or use another account to reach restricted material.

## Content restriction

The checked guidelines restrict direct AI-generated or AI-polished text and set
additional rules for different promotion categories. Do not produce paste-ready
outreach replies or promotional posts for this platform under this skill. Provide
an evidence summary, rule links, and factual questions the user can address in their
own writing. Do not provide screenshot packaging, obfuscation, or "humanization"
as a way to evade the restriction.

If rules change, inspect the exact current text and scope before changing this
adapter's research-only behavior. Do not treat an exception for displaying AI
output as permission for all types of AI-assisted promotion.

## Promotion review

If asked to review the feasibility of a user's independently written promotion,
inspect the applicable category, tag, template, eligibility, and project requirements
in the live guidelines. Open-source, public-benefit, ordinary, and advanced
promotion are distinct. Do not simplify them to "self-promotion is allowed" or
automatically apply one category's permissions to another.
