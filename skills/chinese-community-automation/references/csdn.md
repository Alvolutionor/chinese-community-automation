# CSDN

## Discover

Distinguish technical Q&A, article comments, and community posts. Search the task,
error, or compatibility requirement rather than treating every matching tutorial
as a lead. Read existing solutions and whether the requester still needs help.
Do not access paywalled article bodies or paid resources through unofficial routes.

## Draft

Answer the technical problem directly. A useful response can identify a failed
assumption, a diagnostic check, or a verified tool capability. For an original
article explicitly requested by the user, include real implementation or workflow
evidence and limitations. Avoid an answer that only sends the reader elsewhere.

## Platform-specific checks

Find the current content-creation rules through the official account or help
center. Inspect rules for the specific community and surface. A platform promotion
management feature does not authorize advertising in every answer or comment.
Do not copy another author's article as an affiliated product tutorial.
